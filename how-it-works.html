Will my car pass?

Mass-produced road car, safe and trackworthy, road tyres, working brakes and steering, no major leaks, battery and seat secured. Registration isn’t required. If you’re eyeing something weird, send us photos before you spend the money.

A typical race day

Six sessions, twenty-minute breaks. Refuel, swap drivers, fix whatever broke, eat a sausage.

Timetables vary between venues for noise limits, daylight and circuit operating requirements. Always work from the final schedule issued for your event.

What to wear

Cotton or fire-retardant, neck to wrist to ankle. Fully enclosed leather footwear. Helmet to AS/NZS 1698, Snell or FIA. No shorts, no thongs, no polyester.

Radios

Every car needs a working race radio that can hear Race Control. The frequency is issued before each event. No radio, no track.

Refuelling

Session breaks only, car stationary, no work happening at the same time. Your team supplies a fire marshal with a 4.5kg dry powder extinguisher.
