Entry from $1,000 The ultimate budget challenge

Proper racing.Without the silly budget.

Budget Enduro is Australia’s endurance racing challenge for ordinary cars and ordinary people. Buy something cheap, get three mates together and race it on a proper circuit for a full day.

You don’t need a six-figure race car to have a proper race. Reliability beats speed, and the team that keeps its heap running usually beats the one that doesn’t.

Affordable

Built around a car worth roughly $4,000 and an entry from $1,000 for the whole team. Split it six ways and it’s cheaper than a weekend away.

Real endurance racing

Six one-hour sessions. Teams, strategy, traffic, driver changes, refuelling and proper track time on real Australian circuits.

Anyone can get involved

Grassroots competitors, not professional race teams. Drivers from fourteen up, families, mates and workmates all welcome.
